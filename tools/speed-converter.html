How to Convert Speed

1. Enter the speed you want to convert.

2. Select the original speed unit.

3. Select the unit you want to convert to.

4. Click Convert Speed to see the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert km/h to mph?

Enter your speed, select KM/H as the source unit and MPH as the target unit, then click Convert Speed.

How do I convert mph to km/h?

Enter your speed, select MPH as the source unit and KM/H as the target unit, then click Convert Speed.
