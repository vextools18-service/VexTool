Length Unit Converter

Use the VexTools Unit Converter to convert common length measurements between meters, kilometers, centimeters, feet and inches. Enter a value, select the original unit, choose the target unit and click Convert.

What is a unit converter?

A unit converter changes a measurement from one unit into another equivalent unit. Unit conversion is useful for everyday measurements, education, engineering, construction and many other applications.

Supported length units

This converter supports meters (m), kilometers (km), centimeters (cm), feet (ft) and inches (in).

How to use the Unit Converter

Enter the number you want to convert, select the unit you are converting from, select the unit you want to convert to, and click the Convert button. The converted value will appear instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Unit Converter free?

Yes. The VexTools Unit Converter is completely free to use online.

Can I convert meters to feet?

Yes. Select Meters as the original unit and Feet as the target unit, then click Convert.

Can I convert kilometers to meters?

Yes. Select Kilometers as the original unit and Meters as the target unit to convert the measurement.
