Free Online Image Resizer

VexTools Image Resizer is a free online tool that lets you resize JPG, PNG, WebP and other image files to custom dimensions. Enter your desired width and height, then download the resized image.

The image is processed directly in your browser. No software installation is required and your image does not need to be uploaded to a server.

How to Resize an Image

1. Select an image from your device.

2. Enter the desired width and height in pixels.

3. Keep "Keep aspect ratio" enabled if you want to preserve the image proportions.

4. Click Resize Image.

5. Download your resized image.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize an image online?

Select an image, enter the desired width and height, and click Resize Image. You can then download the resized image.

Is this image resizer free?

Yes. VexTools provides this online image resizer completely free.

Can I keep the original aspect ratio?

Yes. Enable the Keep aspect ratio option to automatically maintain the original proportions when changing the width or height.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image is processed directly in your browser and is not uploaded by this tool.
