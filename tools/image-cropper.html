Free Online Image Cropper

VexTools Image Cropper is a free online tool that lets you crop JPG, PNG, WebP and other image files directly in your browser. Select an image, drag over the area you want to keep, and download the cropped result.

The image is processed locally in your browser, so you do not need to upload your image to a server.

⚡ Fast Crop images directly in your browser.
🆓 Free Use the image cropper without a paid subscription.
🔒 Private Your image stays on your device during processing.

How to Crop an Image

1. Click Select an Image and choose your image.

2. Drag over the image to select the area you want to keep.

3. Click Crop Image.

4. Download your cropped image.
