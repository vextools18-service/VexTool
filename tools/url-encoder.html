What is URL encoding?

URL encoding, also called percent encoding, converts characters that may not be safe or have special meanings in URLs into encoded representations. For example, spaces and certain special characters are represented using percent-encoded values.

How to encode a URL

Enter your URL or text into the input box and click Encode URL. The tool will generate the URL-encoded version instantly.

How to decode a URL

Paste URL-encoded text into the input box and click Decode URL. The original readable text will appear in the result box.
