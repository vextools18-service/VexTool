Frequently Asked Questions

What is a URL slug?

A URL slug is the readable part of a web page URL that describes the page content. For example, "best-online-tools" can be used as a URL slug.

How do I generate an SEO-friendly slug?

Enter your title into the VexTools Slug Generator. The tool converts the text to lowercase, removes unnecessary characters and replaces spaces with hyphens.
