How to Compress a PDF

2. Choose Low, Medium or High optimization.

3. Click the Compress PDF button.

4. Wait for processing to finish.

5. Download your optimized PDF.

What Does PDF Compression Do?

PDF compression can reduce file size by optimizing the internal PDF structure, removing unnecessary metadata and saving the document using more efficient PDF object streams.

The amount of size reduction depends on the original PDF. PDFs that already contain heavily compressed images may show little or no size reduction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install software?

Are my PDF files uploaded?

The PDF processing performed by this page happens locally in your browser. The tool does not intentionally upload your selected PDF to a VexTools server.

Will every PDF become smaller?

Not necessarily. Some PDFs are already highly optimized, so the resulting file may have little size reduction or may even be slightly larger after being rewritten.
