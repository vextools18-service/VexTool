Free Online Base64 Encoder & Decoder

VexTools Base64 Encoder and Decoder is a free online tool that lets you encode normal text into Base64 or decode Base64 strings back into readable text directly in your browser.

What is Base64 Encoding?

Base64 is a method of representing binary data using a limited set of text characters. It is commonly used when data needs to be safely represented as text, such as in web applications, APIs, data URLs and software development.

Encode Text to Base64

Enter any text into the input box and click Encode. VexTools will convert the text into its Base64 representation instantly. Unicode text, including many international characters, is supported.

Decode Base64 to Text

Paste a valid Base64 string into the input box and click Decode. The tool will convert the Base64 data back into readable text.

Is This Base64 Tool Free?

Yes. The VexTools Base64 Encoder and Decoder is completely free to use. The conversion happens directly in your browser, so your text does not need to be uploaded to a server.
