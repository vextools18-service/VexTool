Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 and add 32. For example, 0°C equals 32°F.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature and multiply the result by 5/9.

How do I convert Celsius to Kelvin?

Add 273.15 to the Celsius temperature. For example, 0°C equals 273.15 K.

How do I convert Kelvin to Celsius?

Subtract 273.15 from the Kelvin temperature.
