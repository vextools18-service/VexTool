Frequently Asked Questions

How is the monthly loan payment calculated?

The calculator uses the standard fixed-rate loan payment formula based on the principal amount, monthly interest rate and number of monthly payments.

What happens if the interest rate is 0%?

If the interest rate is 0%, the loan amount is simply divided by the number of monthly payments.

Is this loan calculator free?

Yes. The VexTools Loan Calculator is completely free to use online.

Are the results exact?

The results are estimates. Actual loan payments may vary depending on lender fees, taxes, insurance, compounding methods and other loan terms.
