Free Online Word Counter

VexTools Word Counter is a free online tool that helps you quickly count the number of words in any text. Simply type or paste your content into the text box and the tool instantly calculates your word count.

Along with word counting, this online text counter also shows the total number of characters, characters without spaces, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time. Everything works directly in your browser, making the tool fast and easy to use.

What Can You Count With This Word Counter?

The VexTools Word Counter provides several useful text statistics:

  • Count total words in your text.
  • Count total characters including spaces.
  • Count characters without spaces.
  • Count sentences automatically.
  • Count paragraphs in your text.
  • Estimate reading time based on word count.

Who Can Use an Online Word Counter?

A word counter can be useful for students, writers, bloggers, content creators, researchers, teachers and anyone who needs to check the length of written content. It can help you stay within word limits for assignments, articles, essays, social media posts and other types of content.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a word counter?

A word counter is an online tool that counts the number of words in a piece of text. VexTools also counts characters, characters without spaces, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time.

Does the Word Counter count characters?

How is reading time calculated?

VexTools estimates reading time using approximately 200 words per minute and rounds the result up to the nearest minute.
