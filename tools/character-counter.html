❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a character counter?
A character counter is an online tool that counts the total number of characters in text. VexTools also counts characters without spaces, words and lines.
How do I count characters in text?
Type or paste your text into the VexTools Character Counter. The character count updates automatically as you type.
Does the character counter count spaces?
Yes. The Characters count includes spaces, while the No Spaces count shows the number of characters without spaces or other whitespace.
Can I count words with this tool?
Yes. VexTools Character Counter also provides a real-time word count along with character and line counts.
Is VexTools Character Counter free?
Yes. VexTools Character Counter is free to use online and does not require an account or installation.
Is my text uploaded to a server?
The counting calculations are performed directly in your browser. No account is required to use the tool.
