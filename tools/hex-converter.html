Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert text to HEX?

Enter your text in the input box and click Text → HEX. The hexadecimal result will appear below.

How do I convert HEX to text?

Enter hexadecimal values separated by spaces and click HEX → Text to convert them back into readable text.
