VexTools Hex Converter lets you convert normal text into hexadecimal values and convert hexadecimal values back into readable text. The conversion happens directly in your browser. It supports normal English text as well as Unicode characters such as emojis and other languages.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert text to HEX?

Enter your text in the input box and click Text → HEX. The hexadecimal result will appear below.

How do I convert HEX to text?

Enter hexadecimal values and click HEX → Text to convert them back into readable text.

Is this HEX converter free?

Yes. VexTools provides this hexadecimal converter completely free.

Does it support emojis and other languages?

Yes. The converter uses UTF-8 encoding, so it can correctly handle emojis and Unicode text such as Urdu, Arabic and other languages.
