Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

1. Introduction

Welcome to VexTools. VexTools provides free online tools for tasks such as calculations, text processing, image processing, developer utilities and other useful functions.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use the VexTools website.

2. Information We Collect

VexTools is designed so that many of our tools can be used without creating an account or directly providing personal information.

Depending on how you use the website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by third-party services. This may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate usage information
  • Referring website

3. Information Entered Into Our Tools

Many VexTools utilities perform processing directly in your web browser. When a tool operates entirely within your browser, the information entered into that tool is not intentionally uploaded to VexTools servers.

However, some tools or future features may require external services to perform specific functions. Where applicable, the relevant tool may communicate with third-party services.

Users should avoid entering sensitive, confidential or private information into any online tool unless they understand how that particular tool processes the data.

4. Cookies

VexTools may use cookies or similar technologies provided by third-party services.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as remembering preferences, understanding website usage, measuring performance and displaying relevant advertising.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

VexTools may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense.

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, personalize or measure advertising based on information such as visits to this and other websites.

Google may use advertising cookies to help display ads that are more relevant to users. Users can manage advertising preferences through Google's available advertising settings.

6. Third-Party Services

VexTools may use third-party services for purposes such as website analytics, advertising, security, hosting or other website functionality.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use VexTools. Analytics information can help us improve the website, identify technical problems and develop better tools.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information associated with the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

VexTools does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided to the website by a child, please contact us so that appropriate action can be considered.

10. External Links

VexTools may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or policies of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, services or legal requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how VexTools handles information, you can contact us through the official contact method provided on the VexTools website.
